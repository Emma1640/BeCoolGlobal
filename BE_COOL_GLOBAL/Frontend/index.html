About Be Cool Global

A Ugandan-founded movement for renewal

Be Cool Global is a proposed faith-based nature, wellness and emotional renewal organization, headquartered in Kampala, Uganda, with a long-term global scope.

Our story

Be Cool Global began with a simple observation about the country it comes from: Uganda holds some of the most restorative landscapes on earth, and most of the people who live closest to them never get to rest in them.

So the organization was designed to do two things at once. It creates genuinely excellent nature and wellness travel for people who can pay for it, and it uses that work to fund free and subsidized experiences for people who cannot. Faith is the foundation underneath both — not as a requirement placed on guests, but as the reason the organization exists and the way it treats people.

Vision

To become a global faith-based movement that uses nature, wellness and meaningful travel to inspire renewal, hope and transformation while making restorative experiences accessible to people regardless of their financial ability.

Mission

To create accessible nature-based wellness experiences that promote personal renewal, encourage Christian faith and values, support responsible tourism and extend compassionate service to individuals and communities in need.

Core values

Faith

Our faith-based identity

Be Cool Global is founded on Christian principles: love, compassion, hope, service, stewardship, hospitality, integrity, generosity and respect for human dignity.

Christian activities — gospel music, worship, prayer and Scripture-based reflection — are offered through specifically designed programmes. Taking part in them is always voluntary. We remain authentically Christian while respectfully serving people from different backgrounds, and no guest is required to join a religious activity to belong on a trip.

Our overall aim

To establish a global faith-based organization headquartered in Uganda that uses responsible tourism, natural environments, professional wellness support and voluntary Christian experiences to facilitate personal renewal — while providing free and subsidized services to people who cannot afford them.

Wellness

Support from people qualified to give it

Nature provides the environment. Wellness professionals provide appropriate support. We are careful about the line between the two.

Nature and wellbeing

Time in natural environments changes how people rest, think and relate to their own circumstances. Our programmes are designed so that the setting is doing real work: unhurried days, long light, quiet, water, walking, and enough space to notice what you have been carrying.

Personal renewal

Renewal is not an event. Programmes aim to send people home with practices they can actually keep — ways of resting, reflecting and setting direction that survive contact with ordinary life.

Reflection and development

Guided reflection, journaling and personal development sessions help participants process the experience rather than simply enjoy it and forget it.

Who delivers the support

Be Cool Global works with appropriately qualified professionals, including:

  • Counsellors
  • Psychologists
  • Wellness practitioners
  • Other appropriately qualified professionals

Services may include

  • Wellbeing education
  • Guided reflection
  • Personal development and goal-setting
  • Stress-management education
  • Relaxation activities
  • Group or individual sessions within professional scope

What we do not claim

Be Cool Global does not present tourism as a replacement for medical or mental-health care. Our programmes are restorative experiences, not treatment.

Where specialized care is required, we maintain appropriate referral pathways and will say so plainly. If you are currently under the care of a clinician, we encourage you to discuss any programme with them before booking, and to tell us anything we should know in order to support you well.

Be Cool Renewal Fund

Rest should not depend on income

The Renewal Fund exists to make restorative experiences accessible to people who would otherwise never be able to afford them. It is part of the organization's identity, not an extra programme bolted on the side.

How it works

ONE

Money comes in

From donations, grants, churches, corporate sponsorship, philanthropic contributions, fundraising, and a defined proportion of income from paid programmes.

TWO

Places are allocated

Beneficiaries are identified through applications, community partners, churches and organizations working with the groups the fund exists to serve.

THREE

Impact is reported

We monitor and evaluate programmes, and report on how funds were used and what participants experienced.

Serve is part of the journey

Travel. Connect. Renew. Serve. The last word is not decoration. Every paid place contributes to the system that opens a place for someone else, which is why giving is built into the model rather than requested at the end of it.
